Our story

Bringing the American dream to Morden

We're a family-run business that believes in bold flavours, quality ingredients, and a warm welcome.

Founded in 2020, Janan Trade started with a simple idea: share the authentic taste of American comfort food with our neighbours in Morden. Every recipe is crafted from scratch, using locally sourced produce wherever possible.

Our kitchen is inspired by the smoky pits of Texas, the burger joints of Chicago, and the diners of New York. We're proud to be a part of this community, and we pour our heart into every plate.
